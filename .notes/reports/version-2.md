## 6. Known gaps

Numbered. What is missing or weak, in priority order.

1. Scroll restore on Back from `/card/:id` works at both widths for the first page (1,500 → 1,500) but a deep position comes back clamped to the 100-row page height (11,697 → 4,489) because `CardList` remounts with `limit = 100`; keep `limit` (router `state` or a map keyed by search) or render up to the restored offset.

5. Focus after a route change lands on `body` (phone Enter on a row → detail, laptop Open ↗); no heading focus or announcement. Laptop: 25 Tabs from home to the sidebar "All cards" (sidebar last in DOM; a skip link or sidebar before `main` with `order` would help).
